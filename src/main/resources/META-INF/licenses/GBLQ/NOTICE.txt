orbis-quebec-lakes.tsv.gz is derived from the download index of the Géobase des bathymétries de lacs du Québec (GBLQ),
Gouvernement du Québec (https://www.donneesquebec.ca/recherche/dataset/bathymetries-lacs).

Changes: only lakes with survey data kept, each reduced to its number, zip file name and bounding box in latitude and
longitude (tools/quebec_lakes.py). The lakes' surveys themselves are fetched from the same source when a world needs them.
