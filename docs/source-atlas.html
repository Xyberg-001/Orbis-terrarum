What the research found

Geology

One service covers every country

Macrostrat answers a point query with the rock type and age, using the best map it has for that spot (1:10M worldwide, 1:1M or finer in many countries). About 25 national surveys add 1:25k to 1:250k detail.

Building heights

Norway-grade in about 15 countries

Open lidar surface models: Netherlands, Flanders, Wallonia, France, Spain, Czechia, Estonia, England, Switzerland, New Zealand, Canada, North Rhine-Westphalia, Denmark (free token) and several US states. Spain and Estonia even serve building heights ready-made.

Roads

Widths or lanes in about 12 countries

Finland's Digiroad and France's BD TOPO have both, with no key. The USA has lane counts for every state's main roads; Spain, Brazil, British Columbia, Japan and Switzerland have lanes or width classes.

Photos

About 30 open orthophoto services

Mostly Europe, Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Australian states, Ontario and Québec, several US states and Uruguay. Most are 10 to 25 cm, far sharper than what Orbis uses now.

Lakes

Rare: six places

Switzerland, Finland, Minnesota, Ontario, Saskatchewan and the IJsselmeer. Everywhere else keeps the GLOBathy estimate.

Gaps

Little in Asia, Africa, Latin America

China, Korea and most of South Asia, Africa and Latin America have nothing open, or block foreign networks. They keep the global sources. Several Polish, Portuguese, Balkan and Latin American servers did not answer from your network at all.

Suggested build order

Most services fall into a few kinds of connector. Building a connector once makes each further country mostly a configuration entry. The order puts reach and low cost first.

1

Rock types everywhere

A point-query connector (REST JSON, ArcGIS identify, WMS GetFeatureInfo, WFS) plus a table that turns lithology text in many languages into rocks: granito, Sandstein, kalkstein. Macrostrat alone covers every country; national surveys refine it.

Buildings with heights and floors

Vector services that carry each building's height or storeys (France, Netherlands, Spain, Slovenia, Vienna, New York, San Francisco, Abu Dhabi, Cape Town, Melbourne, Singapore, Japan's PLATEAU), matched to OSM buildings the way the building atlas already is.

Road widths and lanes

The NVDB matcher made general: any service that returns road lines with a width or lane count. Finland, France, USA, Spain, Brazil, British Columbia, Japan, Switzerland, Estonia, Chile.

Before building

Practical issues

  • National map grids. Most European services answer only in their own projection (Netherlands 28992, France 2154, Spain 25830, Czechia 5514, Switzerland 2056 and more). Orbis needs a projection library for step 3.
  • Units. Oregon, Kentucky and New York give heights in feet.
  • Tile order. Japan's geology and Taiwan's photos use z/y/x, the rest z/x/y.
  • Server quirks. IGN France needs STYLES=normal; Flanders' geology answers gzip; Australian and Tasmanian servers refuse plain HTTP clients without a browser-like user agent.

Access and licences

  • Free keys: Denmark, Finland's photos, Sweden, Portugal, New Zealand's basemaps and road data, Korea. A key would be a setting each player fills in.
  • Not usable: Tasmania's photos and EOX's 2018–2025 layers (non-commercial), Britain's OS data (premium), China's Tianditu (blocks foreign networks), Korea's VWorld (seems to block foreign networks).
  • To confirm before shipping: Abu Dhabi, Cape Town, Catastro, Italy's national portal, São Paulo's lidar (labelled GPL-3), South Africa's photo proxy (meant for OSM mapping).
Compiled from eight research passes on 1 October 2026. Orbis already uses Mapterhorn terrain (which includes many of these countries' lidar ground models), ESA WorldCover, Esri imagery, OpenStreetMap, GlobalBuildingAtlas, Open Waters, MET Norway and Open-Meteo; none of those are repeated here.
